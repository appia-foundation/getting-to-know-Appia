# Introduction

![Appia Foundation](assets/appia-logo.png){ width="400" }

**Translating global standards into assessable criteria for the AI value chain.**

The Appia Foundation is an international collaboration formed to build a practical means of
assessing that AI systems meet the obligations and expectations of consumers across the supply
chain. Working with stakeholders across industry, government, and policy, the Appia Foundation
aims to bridge foundational international standards with practical, trusted assessments across
the global AI value chain.

Find out more about the Appia Foundation and how to get involved at
[appiafoundation.org](https://appiafoundation.org/).

This site is your guide to onboarding within the Appia Foundation — governance, membership,
collaboration norms, and how work gets done day to day. Start with whichever page is most
relevant to you, or work through the nav in order for the full picture.
